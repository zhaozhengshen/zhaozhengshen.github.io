Multiobjective Ant Colony System Algorithm for Component-Level Construction Schedule Optimization

构件级施工进度优化的多目标蚁群系统算法
Zhaozheng Shen, Jie Wu
Journal of Construction Engineering and Management 151(3), 04025002
Published已发表

This study models component-level construction scheduling under precedence and resource constraints. A multiobjective ant colony system searches trade-offs among construction duration, cost and carbon emissions, with BIM-based sequence visualization.

该研究建立考虑先后关系与资源约束的构件级施工调度模型，通过多目标蚁群系统搜索工期、成本与碳排放之间的折中方案，并用 BIM 展示施工顺序。

A dual-adaptive directed genetic algorithm for construction scheduling

用于施工调度的双自适应定向遗传算法
Zhaozheng Shen, Jie Wu, Yilun Cao
Journal of Building Engineering 96, 110659
Published已发表

This study extracts constructability constraints from BIM models and uses a dual-adaptive directed genetic algorithm to generate feasible component installation sequences. The published evaluation includes model comparisons and a 1,165-member steel-frame case.

该研究从 BIM 模型提取可建造性约束，使用双自适应定向遗传算法生成满足约束的构件安装顺序。已发表评估包含模型对比和一个 1,165 构件钢框架案例。

Reinforcement Learning–Driven Intelligent Path Planning for Dual Tower Cranes in Modular Construction Sites

模块化施工现场双塔吊强化学习智能路径规划
Shanshan Jiang, Yifan Wang, Bin Yang, Zhaozheng Shen
Proceedings of the 43rd International Symposium on Automation and Robotics in Construction (ISARC 2026)
Conference paper会议论文

A reinforcement-learning framework for coordinated path planning of two tower cranes in a BIM-defined Unity worksite, with bounding-box collision detection and staged curriculum learning for pathfinding, obstacle avoidance and cooperative lifting.

在 BIM 定义的 Unity 施工现场中，构建双塔吊协同路径规划的强化学习框架，结合包围盒碰撞检测，并通过分阶段课程学习完成寻路、避障与协同吊装。

基于智能算法的钢框架结构施工调度方法研究

Research on Construction Scheduling Method for Steel Frame Structures Based on Intelligent Algorithms
Zhaozheng Shen
Master’s thesis, Tongji University; supervisor: Jie Wu
Master’s thesis硕士学位论文

This master’s thesis connects BIM-derived component constraints with sequential, multiobjective and dynamic construction scheduling. It integrates DADGA, MOACS and a Dueling Double DQN with prioritized replay within a steel-frame scheduling workflow.

这份硕士论文将 BIM 构件约束与串行、多目标及动态施工调度关联，整合 DADGA、MOACS 和带优先经验回放的决斗双深度 Q 网络，构建钢框架施工调度流程。
