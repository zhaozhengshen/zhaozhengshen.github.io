I am a PhD student and Research Engineer at the National University of Singapore. My research focuses on learning-based control, dynamics and motion planning for robotic systems, with a particular interest in underactuated tower cranes and autonomous construction equipment.

我是新加坡国立大学的博士研究生和研究工程师，研究方向为机器人系统的学习型控制、动力学与运动规划，尤其关注欠驱动塔吊与自主施工装备。

My current work investigates reinforcement learning and model-based control for rapid load transport, active anti-sway control and stable arrival. I also work on robotic manipulation and geometric perception for automated interior finishing in prefabricated construction.

目前的工作研究用强化学习与基于模型的控制实现快速吊运、主动抑摆与稳定到位；同时参与预制建筑室内装修自动化中的机器人操作与几何感知研究。
