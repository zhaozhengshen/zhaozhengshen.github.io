Learning-Based Control of Underactuated Tower Cranes欠驱动塔吊的学习型控制

A tower crane moves its payload only through a cable: slewing, trolley travel and hoisting are actuated, the payload swing is not. I study controllers that transport the load quickly, suppress sway actively during motion and braking, and bring it to a stable stop at the target under wind.

塔吊只能通过吊绳间接带动吊物：回转、变幅小车与起升可以驱动，吊物摆动不能直接控制。我研究在风扰下既能快速运输、又能在运动和制动中主动抑摆、并在目标处稳定停住的控制器。

  • Unity simulation of an underactuated crane with passive payload swing and windUnity 欠驱动塔吊仿真：被动吊物摆动与风扰
  • Reinforcement-learning policies with three-axis acceleration control三轴加速度控制的强化学习策略
  • Wind fields around tall buildings from CFD, to understand the disturbance用 CFD 研究高层建筑周边风场，理解扰动来源
Background研究背景

A tower crane is a typical underactuated system: the controller can only influence the suspended load indirectly through slewing, trolley travel and hoisting, and cannot act on its swing directly. Rapid acceleration and braking, changes of direction and wind on site all excite coupled motion, so the load drifts from its intended path and keeps swinging after it reaches the target. Existing work has made progress on lift path planning, obstacle avoidance and active anti-sway control. How to combine these capabilities in a cluttered construction site, achieving fast transport, safe obstacle avoidance and a precise stop with little residual swing, remains an open challenge.

塔吊是典型的欠驱动系统：控制器只能通过回转、变幅和起升间接影响悬吊载荷，无法直接控制吊物的摆动。快速加减速、运动方向变化以及施工现场的风扰，都会引发复杂的耦合运动，使吊物偏离预期轨迹，并在到达目标后产生残余摆动。尽管现有研究已在吊运路径规划、避障和主动防摆控制方面取得进展，但如何在复杂施工环境中将这些能力有效结合，同时实现高效运输、安全避障和低残余摆动的精确停靠，仍是一项重要挑战。

Autonomous Visual Data Collection自主视觉数据采集

A robot inspecting a building under construction has to decide where to go and when an image is worth taking. In simulation, a mobile robot learns to move through an indoor floor and capture images so that each step adds useful coverage, while its depth images build up a 3D scan of the floor.

在施工中的建筑里巡检的机器人，需要决定去哪里、何时拍摄才有价值。在仿真中，移动机器人学习在室内楼层中移动与拍摄，使每一步都带来有效覆盖，同时用深度图逐步建立整层楼的 3D 扫描。

  • Reinforcement learning that couples navigation with image acquisition把导航与图像采集结合起来的强化学习
  • Coverage as the objective: useful views, travel cost and repeated captures以覆盖为目标：有效视角、行走成本与重复拍摄
  • Depth images fused into a 3D point cloud to audit what has been seen深度图融合为 3D 点云，用来检查哪些区域已被看到

Robotic Interior Finishing for PPVC ModulesPPVC 模块的机器人室内装修

Interior finishing inside prefabricated (PPVC) modules is repetitive work in tight rooms, and every task depends on the as-built geometry. As Research Engineer on a BCA-funded project, I work on robotic operation sequences for tile installation and floor screeding in simulation, on floor geometry from point clouds, and on task-time analysis of the finishing workflow.

预制（PPVC）模块内的室内装修是在狭小空间里重复进行、且依赖实测几何的作业。作为 BCA 资助项目的研究工程师，我在仿真中研究贴砖与地面找平的机器人作业序列，基于点云分析地面几何，并对装修流程做工时分析。

  • Robotic operation sequences for tile installation and floor screeding (simulation)贴砖与地面找平的机器人作业序列（仿真）
  • Reference planes and local slopes from floor point clouds由地面点云估计基准平面与局部坡度
  • Site investigations and task-time analysis of the finishing workflow装修流程的现场调研与工时分析

Robotic Sorting of Mixed Construction and Demolition Waste混合建筑拆除垃圾的机器人分拣

Construction and demolition waste does not come as clean, separate objects: rebar often still carries concrete, and one grasp can lift two materials at once. In Isaac Sim, a robot arm picks items from mixed piles and sorts them into material bins; I study how to recognise such coupled pieces and where they should go.

建筑拆除垃圾并不是干净、彼此分离的物体：钢筋上常常还连着混凝土，一次抓取可能同时带起两种材料。在 Isaac Sim 中，机械臂从混合料堆中取物并分入不同材料的料箱；我研究如何识别这种粘连在一起的物件，以及它们应该被送到哪里。

  • Grasp-and-place sorting from simulated mixed piles with a Franka armFranka 机械臂从仿真混合料堆中抓取并分拣
  • Coupled objects: a grasp on rebar that also lifts attached concrete粘连物体：抓住钢筋时一同带起的混凝土
  • Routing ambiguous mixed pieces to a review bin instead of a material bin把难以判定的混合物件送入待复核箱，而不是某个材料箱
