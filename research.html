Research研究

My PhD research is on construction robotics and automation: robots that finish interiors of prefabricated modules, collect visual data on their own, lift loads steadily in wind, and understand what to take apart next. My earlier master’s work on intelligent construction scheduling is listed at the end.

我的博士研究方向是施工机器人与建造自动化：在预制模块内完成室内装修的机器人、自主采集视觉数据、在风中平稳吊装，以及判断下一步拆解什么。硕士阶段的智能施工调度研究列在最后。

Tower-crane and site-layout planning塔吊与施工场地联合布置

Choosing cranes and placing temporary facilities across construction stages

跨施工阶段的塔吊选型定位与临时设施布置

A construction site has to place yards, workshops and offices, and choose where to stand which tower cranes, for every stage of the build. This work treats the whole plan as one sequence of decisions and trains a reinforcement-learning policy to generate it end to end, then checks every plan with an independent evaluator.

施工现场在每个阶段都要布置堆场、车间与办公设施，并决定选用哪些塔吊、立在哪里。该研究把整套方案看作一串连续决策，训练强化学习策略端到端生成，并用独立评价程序逐一复核每个方案。

  • One RL policy picks the crane fleet and places all 16 facilities, stage by stage一个强化学习策略逐阶段选定塔吊编队，并布置全部 16 个临时设施

Carrying a part through clutter with online replanning杂乱工作台上的夹持搬运与在线重规划

Grasping a part and carrying it through dense, moving clutter · MuJoCo contact simulation

夹持工件穿过密集且移动的障碍 · MuJoCo 接触仿真

A UR5e with a Robotiq gripper picks a coloured part and carries it across a dense workbench: eleven static groups with random offsets and rotations, plus two carts that drive into the corridors. A* on a collision-checked roadmap gives the route; a two-second look-ahead watches the carts. When the way ahead is blocked the arm brakes and a background thread searches a new route while the physics keeps running; if no corridor is free, it waits.

UR5e 配 Robotiq 夹爪抓起一个彩色工件，穿过密集的工作台：11 组带随机偏移与旋转的静态物件，外加两台会驶入通道的料车。路线由碰撞检查后的路网上的 A* 搜索给出，2 秒前瞻持续监视料车。前方受阻时机械臂先制动，后台线程在物理仿真不停的情况下搜索新路线；没有可通行的通道时则原地等待。

  • UR5e + Robotiq carries a part across 11 cluttered groups and two moving cartsUR5e + Robotiq 夹持工件穿过 11 组杂物与两台移动料车
  • A* on a whole-arm collision-checked roadmap, 2 s look-ahead, asynchronous replanning整臂碰撞检查路网上的 A*、2 秒前瞻、异步重规划
  • Re-routing finished a matched scene in 31.6 s, against 45.1 s when only waiting在同一场景中换路用时 31.6 s，只等待则需 45.1 s

Intelligent construction scheduling for steel-frame structures钢框架结构智能施工调度

BIM-derived component constraints support a connected workflow for steel-frame construction scheduling. The work covers sequential installation with DADGA, resource-constrained trade-offs with MOACS, and dynamic scheduling with a Dueling Double DQN that selects dispatching rules.

BIM 构件约束支持钢框架施工调度的一体化流程。该系列包括 DADGA 串行安装排序、MOACS 资源受限多目标折中，以及通过决斗双深度 Q 网络选择调度规则的动态调度。

  • Constructability constraints derived from BIM — 2,765 for a 1,165-member steel frame从 BIM 自动提取可施工性约束 —— 1,165 构件钢框架共 2,765 条
  • MOACS: duration, cost and carbon trade-offs with three construction teamsMOACS：三个施工班组下权衡工期、成本与碳排放
  • DADGA: feasible installation sequences under BIM constraintsDADGA：满足 BIM 约束的可行安装顺序

Vision-based object sorting with deep reinforcement learning基于视觉与深度强化学习的物体分拣

NUS ME5402 Advanced Robotics · Part 1 group project (Group 5)

新加坡国立大学 ME5402 高级机器人学 · 第一部分小组项目（第 5 组）

A UR5 arm with an RG2 gripper sorts coloured blocks into matching bins. A pixel-wise Q-learning policy (DRLSorting, Bao et al., Robotica 2022) looks at an RGB-D heightmap and decides where to push, grasp and place. I reproduced the published policy in CoppeliaSim and added the perception layer our group proposed: contour-centred grasp points, explicit target-bin placing and no repeat of failed grasps.

UR5 机械臂配 RG2 夹爪，把彩色积木分拣到同色料框。像素级 Q 学习策略（DRLSorting，Bao 等，Robotica 2022）读取 RGB-D 高度图，决定在哪里推、抓与放。我在 CoppeliaSim 中复现了论文公开的策略，并加入小组提出的感知层：按轮廓质心抓取、显式选择目标料框放置、不重复失败的抓取。

  • Reproduced the published DRLSorting policy (Robotica 2022) in CoppeliaSim on the paper’s preset scenes在 CoppeliaSim 中按论文预设场景复现公开的 DRLSorting 策略（Robotica 2022）
  • Added the group’s proposal: contour-centred grasps, explicit target bins, no repeated failed grasps加入小组方案：轮廓质心抓取、显式目标料框、不重复失败抓取
  • Step-by-step Q-value maps and the authors’ learning curves逐步 Q 值图与作者的学习曲线

Modelling and PID control of a wheel-legged robot limb轮腿机器人单腿的建模与 PID 控制

NUS ME5402 Advanced Robotics · Part 2 individual project

新加坡国立大学 ME5402 高级机器人学 · 第二部分个人项目

One limb of a TRON 1-inspired wheel-legged robot is reduced to a planar 3R chain. I derived its D-H model, Jacobian and inverse kinematics, its Lagrange–Euler and Newton–Euler dynamics, simulated open-loop take-off and aerial phases, and designed a gravity-compensated joint-space PID controller that tracks a 30 mm circle with about 1 mm mean error.

将 TRON 1 式轮腿机器人的一条腿简化为平面 3R 串联机构，推导了 D-H 模型、雅可比与逆运动学，以及拉格朗日–欧拉与牛顿–欧拉动力学；仿真了起跳与腾空两个开环阶段，并设计了带重力补偿的关节空间 PID 控制器，使轮轴中心以约 1 mm 的平均误差跟踪 30 mm 圆轨迹。

  • Planar 3R model of one TRON 1-style limb: FK, Jacobian, two-branch IKTRON 1 式单腿的平面 3R 模型：正运动学、雅可比、双解逆运动学
  • Lagrange–Euler and Newton–Euler dynamics; open-loop take-off and aerial phases拉格朗日–欧拉与牛顿–欧拉动力学；开环起跳与腾空阶段
  • Gravity feed-forward cuts mean end-effector error from 227 mm to 1.0 mm重力前馈把末端平均误差从 227 mm 降到 1.0 mm
