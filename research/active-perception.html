Autonomous Visual Data Collection自主视觉数据采集

Learning where to move — and when to look

学习去哪里移动，以及何时观察

How can a robot learn to move and collect images so that each action contributes useful coverage, rather than repeated or ineffective observations?机器人如何学习移动与拍摄，使每次行动带来有用的覆盖，而非重复访问或无效观察？

In simulation, a mobile robot learns where to move through an indoor floor and when to capture images, so that each step adds useful coverage. Depth images from the run are fused into a 3D point cloud of the floor, which is used to check what has and has not been seen.

在仿真中，移动机器人学习在室内楼层中去哪里移动、何时拍摄，使每一步都带来有效覆盖。过程中的深度图融合为整层楼的 3D 点云，用来检查哪些区域已经看到、哪些还没有。

Approach研究方法

Overview概览

Represent what has been observed表达已经观测到的信息

Investigate representations of observed geometry, coverage, and navigation context that support decisions about where to go next.

研究已观测几何、覆盖状态与导航上下文的表达方式，支持下一步移动决策。

Learn movement and acquisition学习移动与采集决策

Explore reinforcement-learning policies that connect navigation with image acquisition, with attention to travel cost, redundancy, and useful views.

探索将导航与图像采集相结合的强化学习策略，同时关注行走成本、重复采集与有效视角。

Test beyond the training layout在训练布局之外检验能力

Examine execution behaviour and coverage in new layouts, using controlled comparisons to separate local improvements from transferable capability.

在新布局中检验执行行为与覆盖能力，通过受控比较区分局部改善与可迁移能力。

How it is evaluated评价方式

  • Useful coverage and difficult-area coverage有效覆盖与难覆盖区域的完成程度
  • Coverage gained per unit of travel or time单位距离或时间带来的覆盖增益
  • Repeated visits and ineffective captures重复访问与无效拍摄
  • Execution reliability and transfer to new layouts执行可靠性与新布局迁移能力

评测路线长 800 m，共 532 个采集点，每个点采集 4 个方向。
