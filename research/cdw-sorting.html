Robotic Sorting of Mixed Construction and Demolition Waste混合建筑拆除垃圾的机器人分拣

Grasping coupled rebar–concrete pieces and deciding where they belong

抓取钢筋与混凝土粘连物，并判断它们该去哪里

When one grasp lifts two materials that are stuck together, how can a sorting robot tell, and where should the piece go?当一次抓取带起两种粘在一起的材料时，分拣机器人如何察觉？这个物件又该送去哪里？

Construction and demolition waste does not come as clean, separate objects: rebar often still carries concrete, and one grasp can lift two materials at once. In Isaac Sim, a Franka arm picks items from mixed piles and sorts them into material bins; I study how to recognise such coupled pieces and where they should go.

建筑拆除垃圾并不是干净、彼此分离的物体：钢筋上常常还连着混凝土，一次抓取可能同时带起两种材料。在 Isaac Sim 中，Franka 机械臂从混合料堆中取物并分入不同材料的料箱；我研究如何识别这种粘连在一起的物件，以及它们应该被送到哪里。

My role:我的工作： Own project (Nov. 2026 – present): simulation, sorting pipeline and analysis.个人项目（2026 年 11 月至今）：仿真、分拣流程与分析。

Overview概览

Simulated mixed piles仿真混合料堆

Piles of wood, rebar, concrete and pipe pieces are generated in Isaac Sim from random seeds, with physics contact between pieces.

在 Isaac Sim 中按随机种子生成由木料、钢筋、混凝土与管材组成的料堆，物件之间有物理接触。

Grasp and place抓取与放置

A Franka arm picks one item at a time and places it in a material bin, observed by a fixed camera.

Franka 机械臂每次抓取一个物件放入材料料箱，由固定相机观测。

Coupled pieces粘连物件

The focus is pieces that bind two materials: recognising when a grasp lifts more than one material, and referring such pieces to a review bin rather than contaminating a material stream.

重点是把两种材料粘在一起的物件：识别一次抓取是否带起了不止一种材料，并把这类物件送入待复核箱，而不是污染某个材料类别。

Process films过程影片

One pile, three outcomes同一料堆，三种结果

The three films follow one development pile, replaying recorded grasp-and-place commands: an ordinary piece goes to its bin, a coupled rebar–concrete piece contaminates the metal bin under the original routing, and the same piece is referred to a review bin instead.

三段影片跟随同一个开发用料堆，回放已记录的抓取与放置指令：普通物件进入对应料箱；按原有路由，钢筋与混凝土的粘连物会污染金属箱；改为把同一物件送入待复核箱。
