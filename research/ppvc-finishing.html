Motivation研究动机

Finishing work inside prefabricated modules预制模块内的装修作业

In Prefabricated Prefinished Volumetric Construction (PPVC), whole room modules are produced and fitted out before they are stacked on site. Interior finishing (wall spraying, tiling and floor screeding) is repetitive and labour-intensive, takes place in tight rooms, and depends on the as-built geometry of each module. The project studies how a mobile manipulator could take over these tasks and what it needs to know about the module to do so.

在预制预装修模块化建造（PPVC）中，整间房间模块在吊装前完成生产与装修。墙面喷涂、贴砖和地面找平等室内装修工作重复、费人工，在狭小空间中进行，并取决于每个模块的实际几何。项目研究移动机械臂如何承担这些工序，以及它需要掌握模块的哪些几何信息。

Geometric perception of the floor地面的几何感知

Floor finishing has to start from the floor as built, not as drawn. From scanned module floors I fit reference planes, estimate local slopes over a moving window and check where water would collect for candidate drain positions, to inform how a robot should level the floor. The analysis is geometric: the vertical is derived from the walls, and ponding is a fill analysis rather than a hydraulic simulation.

地面装修必须从实际建成的地面出发，而不是图纸。我基于模块地面的扫描点云拟合基准平面，用滑动窗口估计局部坡度，并针对候选地漏位置检查可能积水的区域，为机器人找平提供依据。分析是几何层面的：竖直方向由墙面推导，积水为填洼分析而非水力模拟。

Research challenges研究难点

Geometric deviations几何偏差
Each module deviates from its drawings; tiling paths and screed levels have to adapt to the measured surface.每个模块都与图纸存在偏差，贴砖路径与找平标高需要根据实测表面调整。
Task sequencing in tight rooms狭小空间中的工序安排
The robot must finish surfaces without blocking its own way out; screeding has to end at the door.机器人既要完成各个表面，又不能挡住自己的退路；地面找平必须以门口为终点。
Manipulation and motion planning操作与运动规划
Coordinating the mobile base and arm for reach, contact and collision-free motion close to walls.协调移动底盘与机械臂，兼顾可达性、接触与贴墙无碰撞运动。
Interruptions and material handling中断与物料供给
Refilling materials and changeovers break the sequence and must be planned for.补料与换料会打断作业序列，需要纳入规划。
