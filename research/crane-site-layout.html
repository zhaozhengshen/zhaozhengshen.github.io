Tower-crane and site-layout planning塔吊与施工场地联合布置

Choosing cranes and placing temporary facilities across construction stages

跨施工阶段的塔吊选型定位与临时设施布置

Can one learned policy choose the crane fleet and lay out all temporary facilities across construction stages, quickly and without breaking capacity, coverage or geometry rules?能否用一个学习到的策略，跨施工阶段同时决定塔吊编队并布置全部临时设施，既快又不违反吊运能力、覆盖范围与几何约束？

A construction site has to place yards, workshops and offices, and choose where to stand which tower cranes, for every stage of the build. This work treats the whole plan as one sequence of decisions and trains a reinforcement-learning policy to generate it end to end, then checks every plan with an independent evaluator.

施工现场在每个阶段都要布置堆场、车间与办公设施，并决定选用哪些塔吊、立在哪里。该研究把整套方案看作一串连续决策，训练强化学习策略端到端生成，并用独立评价程序逐一复核每个方案。

Overview概览

Before a tower goes up, the site team decides where the material and precast yards, warehouse, workshops, generators and offices go, and which tower cranes stand where in each stage. The two choices are coupled: a yard is only useful inside a crane’s reach, and a crane is only worth its rent if it serves the yards and the building. Classic practice nests them — a genetic algorithm moves facilities and an integer program picks cranes for every candidate. Here a single policy writes the whole plan as one sequence of decisions, with no solver in the loop.

主体结构开工前，现场要确定材料与预制构件堆场、仓库、车间、发电设施和办公区的位置，以及每个阶段选用哪些塔吊、立在哪里。这两件事相互耦合：堆场只有在塔吊覆盖范围内才有用，塔吊只有服务到堆场和建筑才值得它的租金。传统做法把两者嵌套求解：遗传算法移动设施，对每个候选布局再用整数规划选塔吊。本研究让一个策略把整套方案写成一条决策序列，过程中不调用任何求解器。

One decision sequence一条决策序列

The policy picks the crane fleet for each stage, then places and orients all 16 facilities for the preparation and construction layouts, under masks that rule out overlaps and out-of-reach positions.

策略先为每个阶段选定塔吊编队，再为准备期与施工期两套布局依次放置并定向全部 16 个设施；掩码排除重叠与超出吊运范围的位置。

Independent checking独立复核

Every exported plan is rebuilt from the raw site data and re-scored by a separate program: geometry, coverage, crane capacity and cost.

每个导出方案都从原始场地数据重建，由另一套程序重新核验几何、覆盖、塔吊能力与费用。

Operational model (v2)作业层模型（v2）

A stricter model adds multi-crane fleets per stage, integer dispatch of every lift, stage working-hour capacity and look-ahead feasibility checks.

更严格的新版模型加入逐阶段多塔吊编队、逐吊次整数分配、阶段工时容量与前瞻可行性检查。

3Operational model · v2作业层模型 · v2

Several cranes, every lift assigned多台塔吊，每次吊运都有归属

The first model lets one crane cover everything and prices lifting with averages. The stricter v2 model allows up to three cranes per stage, assigns all 36,000 lifts to specific cranes as integers, charges each lift a full slew–trolley–hoist cycle and checks every crane against its working hours. The replay below is the best plan found by a greedy-initialised search for the looser-hours scenario: watch cranes being installed and removed between stages while the per-crane counters fill up.

第一版模型允许一台塔吊覆盖全部需求，并用平均值估算吊运。更严格的 v2 模型每阶段最多三台塔吊，把全部 36,000 次吊运以整数分配给具体塔吊，每次吊运计入完整的回转、变幅与起升周期，并检查每台塔吊的工时容量。下方回放的是在“宽工时”场景下，由贪心初始化搜索找到的最优方案：可以看到塔吊在阶段之间安装与拆除，各塔吊的吊次计数逐渐填满。

What the v2 pilot showsv2 试验说明了什么

  • Under v2 the current policy has not yet produced a valid plan: 0 of 6 scenario pools for both untrained and trained networks; the same-rule greedy pool succeeded in 1.在 v2 下，当前策略尚未生成有效方案：未训练与训练后的网络在 6 个场景池中均为 0；相同规则的贪心池成功 1 个。
  • Three of the six scenarios are provably infeasible under their own assumptions, e.g. tight hours need 2.09 M crane-seconds against at most 1.30 M available.六个场景中有三个在其自身假设下可证明不可行，例如紧工时场景需要 209 万塔吊秒，而最多只有 130 万。
  • Most failures happen when choosing cranes or deciding to stop, so the next step is look-ahead completion checks and bounded backtracking, not a larger network.大多数失败发生在选塔或决定停止时，因此下一步是补充前瞻完成性检查与有限回溯，而不是扩大网络。

v2 runs use the case-study geometry with explicitly assumed operating parameters (cycle speeds, working hours, load weights). They are a model pilot, not a field calibration.v2 试验使用案例场地几何，作业参数（周期速度、工时、吊重）为明确声明的假设，属于模型试验而非现场校准。

Status进展

Status and scope研究进展与适用边界

  • One site geometry, from an existing case-study report; the 64 unseen scenarios vary costs and demand, not the site. Generalisation to new sites is untested.只有一个场地几何，来自既有案例报告；64 个未见场景改变的是费用与需求，而非场地本身。对新场地的泛化尚未测试。
  • Costs are model values in the report’s currency units, including opportunity-cost proxies; they are not a project budget.费用为模型值，沿用报告的货币单位并含机会成本代理，不是项目预算。
  • No dynamic anti-collision between cranes, detailed lift scheduling, road routing or foundation design.未建模塔吊间动态防碰撞、详细吊装排程、道路绕行与基础设计。
End-to-end RL can already write a complete, valid site plan in a few hundredths of a second on the simpler model. The open question is the stricter one: whether look-ahead feasibility checks let a learned policy keep that speed once every lift, every crane-hour and every stage boundary has to add up.在较简单的模型上，端到端强化学习已能在几百分之一秒内写出完整且有效的场地方案。尚待回答的是更严格的问题：当每次吊运、每个塔吊工时和每个阶段边界都必须对得上时，前瞻可行性检查能否让学习策略保住这种速度。
