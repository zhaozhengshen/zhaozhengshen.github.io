Learning-Based Control of Underactuated Tower Cranes欠驱动塔吊的学习型控制

Rapid payload transport, active anti-sway control and stable arrival

快速吊运、主动抑摆与稳定到位

Can a learning-based controller move a suspended load efficiently while actively limiting swing, overshoot, and residual motion?基于学习的控制器能否在高效吊运悬吊载荷的同时，主动抑制摆动、超调与残余运动？

A tower crane moves its payload only through a cable: slewing, trolley travel and hoisting are actuated, the payload swing is not. I study controllers that transport the load quickly, suppress sway actively during motion and braking, and bring it to a stable stop at the target under wind.

塔吊只能通过吊绳间接带动吊物：回转、变幅小车与起升可以驱动，吊物摆动不能直接控制。我研究在风扰下既能快速运输、又能在运动和制动中主动抑摆、并在目标处稳定停住的控制器。

Motivation研究动机

A fast lift that arrives swinging is not finished运得快但到了还在晃，等于没运完

Modular and prefabricated construction relies on tower cranes to place ever heavier modules with high precision. A suspended module that is still swinging when it reaches its target cannot be installed: the crew waits for it to settle or steadies it by hand with tag lines, which costs time and puts people close to a moving load. On high-rise sites, wind makes the swing worse. Operators handle this through experience; I want to know whether a controller can move the load fast and bring it to rest, reliably and under disturbances.

模块化与装配式建造越来越依赖塔吊精确吊装更重的模块。吊物到达目标时如果还在摆动，就无法安装：工人只能等它停稳，或者用牵引绳手动稳住，既耗时又让人靠近运动中的重物。在高层项目上，风会让摆动更严重。司机靠经验处理这些问题；我想知道，控制器能否既把吊物运得快，又能在扰动下可靠地让它停稳。

Background研究背景

An underactuated system in a cluttered site复杂施工环境中的欠驱动系统

A tower crane is a typical underactuated system: the controller can only influence the suspended load indirectly through slewing, trolley travel and hoisting, and cannot act on its swing directly. Rapid acceleration and braking, changes of direction and wind on site all excite coupled motion, so the load drifts from its intended path and keeps swinging after it reaches the target.

塔吊是典型的欠驱动系统：控制器只能通过回转、变幅和起升间接影响悬吊载荷，无法直接控制吊物的摆动。快速加减速、运动方向变化以及施工现场的风扰，都会引发复杂的耦合运动，使吊物偏离预期轨迹，并在到达目标后产生残余摆动。

Existing work has made progress on lift path planning, obstacle avoidance and active anti-sway control. How to combine these capabilities in a cluttered construction site, achieving fast transport, safe obstacle avoidance and a precise stop with little residual swing, remains an open challenge.

尽管现有研究已在吊运路径规划、避障和主动防摆控制方面取得进展，但如何在复杂施工环境中将这些能力有效结合，同时实现高效运输、安全避障和低残余摆动的精确停靠，仍是一项重要挑战。

The problem问题

Three actuated axes, one passive pendulum三个驱动轴，一个被动摆

Slewing rotates the jib, trolley travel moves the suspension point along it and hoisting changes the rope length. The payload is not actuated: it hangs as a pendulum below the trolley, and every motion of the crane excites its swing. Accelerating hard shortens the lift but makes the load swing; braking late makes it overshoot; and wind keeps pushing it in ways the controller cannot measure in advance. The controller therefore has to trade speed against sway over the whole lift, with three goals: rapid transport, active anti-sway during motion and braking, and an accurate arrival with little residual oscillation.

回转带动起重臂转动，变幅小车沿臂移动吊点，起升改变绳长。吊物本身不受驱动，作为摆挂在小车下方，塔吊的每个动作都会激起摆动。加速越猛运得越快但摆得越大；制动过晚就会冲过目标；风还在不断推动吊物，而控制器无法事先测得。因此控制器要在整个吊运过程中权衡速度与摆动，目标有三个：快速运输、运动与制动中的主动抑摆、准确到位且残余摆动小。

Disturbance扰动

Wind near tall buildings高层建筑周边的风

To understand the disturbance, I simulated the wind around a generic 200 m tower with CFD (steady RANS, OpenFOAM) and let a crane on the roof slew a 28 t module around it, without any anti-sway control. The same approaching wind is stronger at the corners, almost calm in the wake and changes abruptly over the roof edge. When the module passes the corner and enters the wake, the wind load on it drops within a few seconds, and that sudden release leaves it swinging noticeably more than in the same wind without the building. A controller that only sees steady, uniform wind in training would not be ready for this.

为了理解扰动，我用 CFD（稳态 RANS，OpenFOAM）模拟了一座 200 m 通用塔楼周围的风场，并让屋顶上的塔吊带着 28 t 模块绕楼回转（不加任何抑摆控制）。同样的来流，在角部更强、尾流中几乎无风、屋顶边缘突变。模块经过角部进入尾流时，风荷载在几秒内骤降，这种突然“释放”使它的摆动明显大于没有建筑时同样风速下的情况。只在稳定、均匀的风中训练的控制器，难以应对这种情况。

Read together, the three animations show why the disturbance matters for control: along one slewing path a load passes through faster, slower and almost calm air within seconds, at every height of the lift.三段动画放在一起看，可以说明扰动对控制的意义：沿同一条回转路径，吊物在几秒内先后经过更强、更弱和几乎无风的区域，而且在吊运的每个高度都是如此。
